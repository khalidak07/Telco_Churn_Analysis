# Telco_Churn_Analysis

# Executive Summary: “Telco Customer Churn Analysis”

This analysis provides an in-depth understanding of customer churn behavior at a telecommunications company, focusing on key data-driven insights and visualizations.

## Data Preparation:
•	Addressed missing values in the TotalCharges column by replacing blanks with zero and converting the column to a numeric format, ensuring accurate calculations.
•	Enhanced data interpretability by converting the SeniorCitizen field from binary (0/1) to descriptive labels ("Yes/No").
Descriptive Statistics:
•	Performed data validation to identify and resolve null values and duplicates.
•	Leveraged summary statistics to understand customer demographics, tenure, and billing patterns.

## Key Findings from Visualizations:
1.	Churn Distribution: 
o	A count plot revealed the proportion of customers who churned versus those who stayed, highlighting churn as a significant issue.
o	The annotated bar chart provided clear visibility into the churn counts.
2.	Churn Percentage: 
o	A pie chart displayed the churn rate as a percentage of the total customer base, offering a quick visual representation of customer retention challenges.

## Business Implications:
•	The analysis identifies churn as a critical metric that needs addressing to improve customer retention.
•	Visual insights suggest potential patterns or segments that may require targeted interventions, such as improved customer service or tailored offers for high-risk groups.
The combination of data preparation, statistical insights, and effective visualizations provides a comprehensive foundation for strategizing churn reduction efforts.
